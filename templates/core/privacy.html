Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

The Local Government Unit (LGU) of Bontoc uses this service to receive and process tricycle franchise applications and renewals and to let applicants check their application status. This policy explains what information the service handles and how it is used.

Information handled

Depending on the service you use, the LGU may handle:

  • Applicant or franchise-holder name, address, contact number, and email address;
  • Vehicle and franchise details, including identifying numbers and renewal information;
  • Supporting documents you upload, application status, and related correspondence; and
  • Information you provide when looking up a franchise or managing an application.

How information is used

Information is used to receive, verify, evaluate, and process applications and renewals; maintain franchise and application records; review documents; communicate updates or requests; and provide status and resubmission functions. Contact details may be used to send application-related notifications.

Access, sharing, and protection

Application information and uploaded documents are available to authorized LGU personnel for official processing. Information may be handled by service providers supporting the operation of this service or delivery of notifications, and may be disclosed where required or authorized by law. The LGU applies safeguards intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

Records are kept as needed to process applications and administer franchise services, and are retained or disposed of in accordance with applicable government records-retention requirements and law.

Reference-code access

A reference code can provide access to the related application's status and management functions without a conventional client account. Anyone with the code may be able to access that application, so keep it confidential and do not share it publicly. Avoid using a shared or public device when viewing application information, and close the page when finished.

Your privacy rights and questions

You may ask the LGU about personal information associated with your application, or raise a correction or privacy concern, by contacting or visiting the Local Government Unit of Bontoc office. Requests may be subject to verification and applicable legal or records-retention requirements. Rights and remedies under applicable privacy laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, remain available.
